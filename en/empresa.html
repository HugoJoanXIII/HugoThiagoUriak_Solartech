Who are we?

SolarTech is a company dedicated to renewable energies and the installation of solar panels.

Our goal is to help build a more sustainable future using clean energy.

We are committed to innovation, sustainability and respect for the environment.

The creators of SolarTech

A group of students from Joan XIII who, thanks to a school project, managed to create SolarTech.

Their main idea is to harness the sun’s energy to improve sustainability and help the environment.

The project aims to raise awareness about the importance of renewable energies.
